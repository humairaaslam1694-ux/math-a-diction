Academic Profile

About Dr. Humaira Aslam

Dr. Humaira Aslam serves as an Assistant Professor of Mathematics, holding a PhD from Adamas University, Kolkata. Her primary research contributions lie in mathematical biology, epidemiological modeling, and non-linear dynamical systems, published across reputable, international peer-reviewed journals.

"Mathematics is built on conceptual foundation, not surface-level formulas. Dr. Aslam guides students through complex analytical frameworks by building deep structural understanding. Every session is designed to transform abstract concepts into clear, usable insight. Her international instructional experience bridges university degree standards (BSc, MSc, Engineering Mathematics), post-graduate entrance examinations (CSIR-NET, GATE, CAT, SAT, GMAT, GRE), and standardized global qualifications including the IB Diploma, Cambridge A-Levels, AP Calculus, and state secondary certificates."
